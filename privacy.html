Privacy Policy

The Long Run Home · Effective 27 November 2026

In short: this game does not collect, store or share any personal information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking, no chat and no in-game purchases. Progress is saved only on your own device.

Who this policy is for

The Long Run Home is a free game played in a web browser. It is made for children and families, and may be played by children under 13. This policy explains what happens to information when anyone plays it. It is written to meet children's privacy laws such as COPPA in the United States and the GDPR (including its rules for children) in the UK and EU.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no server of its own and never sends anything about players to us or to anyone else. We cannot see who plays, how they play or anything they type.

What is saved on your device

So that a player can carry on where they left off, the game saves a small amount of data in the browser's own storage (called localStorage) on the device being used:

  • which stages are unlocked, stars and coins earned, and the best distance in endless mode;
  • settings: which runners are chosen, the difficulty, and whether sound is on;
  • any names typed for the runners (up to 16 characters).

This data never leaves the device and is not sent to us. We recommend that children use made-up names for their runners rather than their real names. To delete everything the game has saved, clear the site's data in the browser's settings (for example, "Clear browsing data" or "Website data" for this site). The game uses no cookies.

The one service that sees basic connection details

Like any website, the game has to be delivered by a web host. The host receives technical details such as your device's IP address and browser type, which it needs in order to deliver the page. We do not control or receive this information.

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the game. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Everything the game needs, including its fonts, is delivered from that host. The game contacts no other website.

The game does not use analytics, advertising, social media plugins or any other trackers.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it does not need or ask for a parent's consent to collect any. It has no way for a child to contact strangers, share information publicly or make purchases. We still recommend that a parent or guardian supervises young children while they play online.

Your rights

Privacy laws give you rights to see, correct or delete personal information an organisation holds about you. We hold none. Everything the game keeps is on your own device, under your control, and you can delete it at any time as described above.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before that change goes live. We will not start collecting personal information from children without first updating this policy and getting any consent the law requires.

Contact

A parent, guardian or anyone else with a question about this policy can contact the game's owner by opening an issue at github.com/tanilancc/stickgirlrun/issues. Please do not include any personal information about a child in an issue.
